Our story

A home kitchen, not a commercial one.

ZeeJay's started the way most good food does — cooking for family, then for neighbours, then for anyone who heard about it.

How we cook

Made when you order, not before.

We don't run a commercial line, and we don't pre-cook trays to reheat later. Every order goes into the kitchen once it's placed, which is exactly why we ask for 6 hours to a day's notice — that's the time it takes to do it properly, from fresh ingredients to your door.
